Privacy Policy for Idle Farm

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Idle Farm ("the Game") is developed and provided by Tableforge. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you use Idle Farm.

1. Information Stored by the Game

Idle Farm may store game progress and gameplay-related information locally on your device.

The Game does not currently require users to create an account and does not currently operate its own online database for player data or game progress.

2. Advertising

Idle Farm may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information in order to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertisements.

This information may include:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Information related to advertisement interactions

The collection and processing of this information is governed by the privacy policies of the respective advertising providers.

For more information about how Google handles data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy:

Google Privacy Policy

3. Rewarded Advertisements

Idle Farm may offer optional rewarded advertisements. Users may voluntarily choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward.

Watching rewarded advertisements is optional.

4. Data Sharing

Tableforge does not sell personal information.

The Game does not currently transmit game progress to servers operated by Tableforge.

However, third-party services used by the Game, such as advertising providers, may process information as described in their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Idle Farm is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Advertising and data processing by third-party providers may be subject to additional protections and requirements depending on the user's age and location.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Game, new features, legal requirements, or changes to third-party services.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
