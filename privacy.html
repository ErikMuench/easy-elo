Privacy Policy for Easy Elo

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Easy Elo ("the App") is developed and provided by Tableforge.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you use Easy Elo.

1. Information Stored by the App

Easy Elo allows users to create and manage player profiles, ratings, and match results.

Currently, player information, ELO ratings, and match history created within the App are stored locally on the user's device.

Easy Elo does not currently require users to create an account, and the App does not currently operate its own online database for player information or rankings.

2. Advertising

Easy Elo may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information in order to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertisements.

This information may include:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Information related to advertisement interactions

The collection and processing of this information is governed by the privacy policies of the respective advertising providers.

For more information about how Google handles data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Data Sharing

Easy Elo does not sell personal information.

The App does not currently transmit player profiles, ELO ratings, or match history to servers operated by Tableforge.

However, third-party services used by the App, such as advertising providers, may process information as described in their respective privacy policies.

4. Future Online Features

Easy Elo may introduce optional online features in the future, such as online leaderboards, cloud synchronization, accounts, or other online services.

If such features require the collection, storage, or processing of additional user information, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before those features become available.

5. Children's Privacy

Easy Elo is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If future features introduce the collection of personal information, appropriate measures and privacy information will be provided where required.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App, new features, legal requirements, or changes to third-party services.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
